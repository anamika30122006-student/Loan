Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Get answers to common queries regarding CreditSamarth scheme applications

1. What is CreditSamarth Portal? ➕
CreditSamarth is a digital one-stop portal connecting Government Credit Schemes directly to borrowers and 300+ partner commercial banks for online eligibility checking, application, and instant in-principle approval.
2. How do I check eligibility for government schemes? ➕
Click on "Check Eligibility" on any scheme card, answer a few basic details about your business type, income, and location. Our AI engine automatically matches you with the best government subsidy scheme.
3. Are loans on CreditSamarth collateral-free? ➕
Yes! Schemes like Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) up to ₹10 Lakhs and PMEGP up to ₹25 Lakhs provide 100% collateral-free credit guarantees under Govt. of India credit guarantee trusts (CGTMSE).
4. How long does digital sanction take? ➕
In-Principle Sanction Letters are generated digitally within 5 to 10 minutes upon successful Aadhaar e-KYC, PAN validation, and document upload verification.
