Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

Nodal Ministry: Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Govt. of India

Apply for Scheme ➔

About the Scheme

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) is a flagship scheme of Government of India to enable a micro enterprise to secure credit up to ₹10 Lakhs without any collateral guarantee. Loans are provided by Banks, NBFCs, and MFIs.

Key Highlights & Financial Assistance

MAXIMUM CREDIT LIMIT
₹ 10,00,000
Shishu (up to ₹50k), Kishor (₹50k-₹5L), Tarun (₹5L-₹10L)
SUBSIDY / GUARANTEE COVER
100% Collateral-Free
Covered under CGTMSE Trust

Who Can Apply?

  • ✓ Individual micro-entrepreneurs, proprietary firms, partnerships, or LLPs.
  • ✓ Age of applicant must be minimum 18 years and maximum 65 years.
  • ✓ Non-farm sector income generating activities in manufacturing, trading, or services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is collateral security required for this scheme? ➕
No. As per Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines, banks do not demand collateral security for loans under this central government credit scheme.
Which banks offer sanction for this scheme? ➕
All 300+ partner lenders registered on CreditSamarth — including State Bank of India, Punjab National Bank, Bank of Baroda, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Regional Rural Banks & SIDBI.
