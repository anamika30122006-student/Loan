📖 STEP-BY-STEP APPLICATION WORKFLOW

The End-to-End Digital Process

Simple 4-stage digital workflow linking micro entrepreneurs to 300+ partner commercial banks with 100% transparency and tracking.

💰

1. Journey Start

Entrepreneur lands on platform and begins loan application.
📜

2. Scheme Select

Choose best suitable scheme (MUDRA, PMEGP, PMFME, CMEGP).
🔍

3. Eligibility Check

AI evaluates eligibility score and matches pre-approval likelihood.
📁

4. Document Upload

Upload required Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Statement, Udyam & GST files.

⚡ Stage 1 & 2: Instant Scheme Selection

Applicants specify their business activity (Retail, Food Processing, Manufacturing, Agriculture) and turnover. Our AI engine recommends exact subsidy schemes with maximum margin money benefits.

🤖 Stage 3 & 4: AI Risk Audit & Sanction

Real-time OCR extraction verifies uploaded Aadhaar, PAN, and Bank Statements. In-Principle Sanction Letter is issued digitally within minutes across 300+ partner lenders.
